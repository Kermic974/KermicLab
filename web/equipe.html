The team (English summary)

The six people presented here are artificial-intelligence agents. They work behind the scenes to run and improve KermicLab, in the service of children, parents and teachers.

  • Émile Grok, KermicLab orchestrator. Émile coordinates all of the KermicLab agents. He assigns the tasks, watches over the consistency of the work and looks for ways to improve the processes: development, content, documentation, monitoring and synchronisation. Key skills: overall view of the project, orchestration, synthesis, communication between agents.
  • Inès Grok, agent auditor. Inès monitors, analyses and audits the activity of the other agents, to guarantee the quality, consistency and effectiveness of their work. Key skills: analysis of activity reports, detection of gaps, clear reports, continuous improvement.
  • Chloé Grok, KermicLab Contenu Tunnel. Chloé creates and refines the content that presents KermicLab, from the first discovery through to the decision, staying aligned with the educational vision, respect for privacy and the freemium offer. Key skills: writing, educational storytelling, adaptation to each step of the information journey, brand consistency.
  • Paul Grok, KermicLab Notion Docs. Paul keeps the KermicLab Notion documentation up to date, by synchronising it with the project's GitHub repository. Key skills: document structuring, GitHub and Notion synchronisation, clarity of writing, continuous maintenance.
  • Hugo Grok, KermicLab GitHub-Grok Sync. Hugo keeps things consistent, in both directions, between the GitHub repository kermic974/kermiclab and the Grok working environment. He watches for updates and makes sure that the files, the documentation and the code stay aligned. Key skills: Git, synchronisation, detection of divergences, maintenance of consistency.
  • Manon Grok, KermicLab Veille Marché. Manon carries out competitive and market monitoring for KermicLab, around interactive educational solutions and the French curriculum (CM1, CM2, 6e and beyond). She draws positioning analyses and recommendations from it. Key skills: monitoring, competitive analysis, strategic synthesis, actionable recommendations.
